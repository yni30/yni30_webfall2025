Early Game Gear:

The main goal in the early game is to get enough gear to last you to the end, and ensure you don't die, especially in the nether. Typically, if a player is not speedrunning, iron will be the main used armor and tools. If you happen to find diamonds, keep them and get them (of course)! Key items are Water Bucket, Shield, Pickaxe, Bow, and Sword (unless playing on Java, which sometimes you will want an axe).

The Nether: Speedrunning portal and loot

Here is how to speedrun a portal: The back three vertical dirt blocks aren't needed.

Once you get into the nether, the main goal is to get pearls and blaze rods for getting to the stronghold. Any other loot isn't needed, but if you find it take it! DEFINITELY TAKE NETHERITE UPGRADE TEMPLATES!!!! To get blaze rods, find a fortress and kill blazes. For pearls, you can either go to a warped forest and kill enderman manually, or trade with piglins for pearls. Make sure to wear a gold helmet (or armor piece, but gold helmet is equal to iron). Finding bastions is very useful, because of the abundance of gold and loot (you can find many arrows in chests, helpful for the end fight).

The end: The dragon, and end cities

For the end, you will have to craft eyes of ender to locate the stronghold, and dig into the stronghold. Bringing a bed will help in case you die. To kill the dragon, there are two main methods. The first is much more dangerous but faster, which is to bed bomb the dragon. When the dragon perches, you can place beds and explode them to deal large amounts of damage. The second option is to take a while and explode the crystals by shooting them, then shooting the dragon when it isn't perched and hitting it using a sword when it IS perched. The dragon drops a lot of XP, but you gain levels more efficiently at lower levels. Bringing an enchant table that is maxed (15 bookshelves) is a good idea! Also collect the dragon egg (it is a massive flex on multiplayer). Then, locate and end gateway and loot end cities. You want to find ones with a floating ship, as those have elytra, one of the best items in the game. Also get ender chests!

Miscellaneous items that will help you:

The main thing to do now is refine your gear. Get full netherite, with the following enchants:

  • Protection 4
  • Unbreaking 3
  • Mending (From Villager)
  • Aqua Affinity (Helmet)
  • Swift Sneak (Leggings)
  • Respiration 3 (Helmet)
  • Depth Strider 3 (Boots)
  • Feather Falling 4 (Boots)
For your sword, get:
  • Sharpness 5
  • Fire Aspect 2
  • Unbreaking 3
  • Looting 3
  • Mending
  • Knockback (Optional)
    For your axe, get:
  • Unbreaking 3
  • Efficiency 5
  • Sharpness 5
  • Mending
    For your pickaxe, get:
  • Unbreaking 3
  • Mending
  • Efficiency 5
  • Fortune 3, OR Silk Touch
Some other things to get are experience bottles (from cleric villagers), potions (specifically Strength II, Swiftness II, Regeneration II, and Turtle Master. Invisibility is optional but sometimes preferred, and DEFINITELY have fire resistance). Ender Pearls are useful for escaping, and a trident and mace is very useful too, depending on the situation. Cobwebs are good for trapping players, and suspicious stew is a good food source (use either dandelions or oxeye daisies for either saturation or regen).
